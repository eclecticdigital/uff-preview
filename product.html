How to measure

Use a soft tape, over a thin layer

  1. Shoulder: from the edge of one shoulder to the other, across the back.
  2. Bust: around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.
  3. Waist: around the narrowest part, usually just above the belly button.
  4. Hip: around the fullest part, feet together.
  5. Length: from the top of the shoulder, next to the collar, down to where you want the hem.
